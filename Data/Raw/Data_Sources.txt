DATA SOURCES
Replication of Diebold and Li (2006), "Forecasting the term structure of
government bond yields", using South African government bond data.
Module: DDM8X01 Debt Market Modelling, Empirical Replication Project
Data downloaded: 27 September 2026

All raw files in this folder are stored exactly as downloaded. All cleaning,
date alignment, frequency conversion and sample selection is done in the
Python code (see the Code folder).

======================================================================
FILE 1: SARB_govt_bond_yields_monthly.xlsx
======================================================================
Source:      South African Reserve Bank (SARB), Online Statistical Query
             (Quarterly Bulletin historical time series)
URL:         https://www.resbank.co.za/en/home/what-we-do/statistics/releases/online-statistical-query
Method:      Manual download, Excel format, original file name
             "TSReport-2026-9-27-16-1-11.xlsx", renamed for clarity
Frequency:   Monthly (monthly average of daily yields)
Unit:        Percentage per annum
Range downloaded: January 1990 to May 2026 (latest available)
Missing values:   Recorded by SARB as "undefined"

Series:
Code      SARB description (as supplied, truncated by SARB)                 Maturity band        Available from
KBP2000M  Yield on bonds traded on the stock exchange: Government bonds -   0 to 3 years         1960/01
          Nominal yield
KBP2001M  Yield on loan stock traded on the stock exchange: Government      3 to 5 years         1985/01
          bonds - 3 to 5 years
KBP2002M  Yield on bonds traded on the stock exchange: Government bonds -   5 to 10 years        1986/05
          Nominal yield
KBP2003M  Yield on bonds traded on the stock exchange: Government bonds -   10 years and over    1960/01
          Nominal yield
KBP2049M  Yield on bonds traded on the stock exchange: Government bonds -   20 to 30 years       2017/01
          Nominal yield

Note on maturity bands: SARB truncates the descriptions in the download.
The maturity band of each series was identified from SARB's published series
definitions, confirmed by matching each series' start date to the SARB
maturity-band series start dates (KBP2000M 1960/01, KBP2001M 1985/01,
KBP2002M 1986/05, KBP2003M 1960/01).

Excluded: KBP2027M (real yield, inflation-linked bonds), since the model uses
nominal yields.

======================================================================
FILE 2: SARB_treasury_bills_daily.csv
======================================================================
Source:      South African Reserve Bank (SARB), web data service
             (market rates / selected historical rates)
URL:         https://custom.resbank.co.za/SarbWebApi/WebIndicators/Shared/GetTimeseriesObservations/{code}/{start date}/{end date}
Method:      Automated download with Code/00_download_tbills.ipynb,
             one calendar year per request, saved unchanged as CSV
Frequency:   Daily observations; the rate changes at each weekly
             Treasury bill auction
Unit:        Percentage per annum
SARB definition: "The average rate at which [n] day Treasury Bills are
             allotted during the weekly Treasury Bill auction"

Series:
Code      SARB series name                               Observations  First date   Last date
MMRD203A  Treasury bills - 91 day (tender rates)         10641         1990-01-02   2026-09-23
MMRD206A  Treasury bills - 182 day (tender rates)        6997          2002-01-02   2026-09-23
MMRD209A  Treasury bills - 273 day (tender rates)        5472          2007-02-13   2026-09-23
MMRD212A  Treasury bills - 364 day (tender rates)        4454          2010-06-30   2026-09-23

Columns in the file: Code, Series, Timeseries, Description, Period, Value
